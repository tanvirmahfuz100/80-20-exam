#### Question 1
The superficial Inguinal ring is an opening in the:

- A Internal oblique aponeurosis
- B Conjoint tendon
- C External oblique aponeurosis
- D Fascia transversalis

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** The superficial inguinal ring is a slit-like opening in the aponeurosis of the external oblique muscle, located just above and medial to the pubic tubercle.

#### Question 2
Which of the following type of necrosis is seen in acute myocardial infarction?

- A Caseous necrosis
- B Gangrenous necrosis
- C Coagulative necrosis
- D Fat necrosis

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Acute myocardial infarction causes coagulative necrosis due to ischemia. The cell architecture is preserved for several days, unlike other necrosis types.

#### Question 3
Main seat of peripheral resistance is:

- A Arteriole
- B Metaarteriole
- C Venule
- D Capillary

**Correct Answer:** A

**Explanation:** Arterioles are the primary site of peripheral resistance due to their thick muscular walls and small diameter (approximately 30-50 microns), allowing significant regulation of blood flow and pressure.

#### Question 4
Blood group antigens are:

- A Present in the Hb molecules
- B Present in the dense granules of platelets
- C Present in the RBC membrane
- D Present in the plasma

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** ABO and Rh blood group antigens are glycoproteins and glycolipids present on the surface of red blood cell membranes, not inside the cell or in plasma.

#### Question 5
Signs of upper motor neuron lesion include all except:

- A Weakness or paralysis of limb
- B Hypertonia of muscles
- C Loss of all reflexes
- D Plantar response extensor

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Upper motor neuron lesions cause hyperreflexia (increased deep tendon reflexes), not loss of reflexes. Loss of all reflexes is seen in lower motor neuron lesions or spinal shock.

#### Question 6
Neonates may be infected with except:

- A Brucellosis
- B Hepatitis B virus
- C Malaria
- D Shigellosis

**Explanation:** Brucellosis is rare in neonates and typically transmitted through unpasteurized milk or direct contact with infected animals. Hepatitis B, malaria, and shigellosis can be transmitted perinatally or postnatally.

#### Question 7
Insulin increases:

- A Lipolysis
- B Ketogenesis
- C Glycogenesis
- D Gluconeogenesis

**Correct Answer:** C

**Explanation:** Insulin promotes glycogen synthesis (glycogenesis) in the liver and muscles. It inhibits lipolysis, ketogenesis, and gluconeogenesis, thereby lowering blood glucose.
