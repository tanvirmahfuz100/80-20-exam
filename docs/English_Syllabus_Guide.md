# English Education and Admission Syllabus Guide (Bangladesh)

This document provides a comprehensive overview of the NCTB syllabus for SSC and HSC English (1st and 2nd Papers), as well as the English syllabus for the Institute of Business Administration (IBA), University of Dhaka admission test.

---

## 1. SSC English Syllabus (NCTB)

### English 1st Paper (Subject Code: 107)
The 1st paper focuses on reading and writing skills, primarily based on the 'English for Today' textbook.

**Part A: Reading Test (50 Marks)**
* **Seen Passage 1:** Multiple Choice Questions (MCQ) and Answering Questions (Open-ended).
* **Seen Passage 2:** Information Transfer or Gap filling without clues.
* **Unseen Passage:** Summarizing and Matching (Table/Sentences).
* **Cloze test with clues:** Vocabulary and grammar-based.
* **Cloze test without clues:** Vocabulary and grammar-based.
* **Rearranging:** Putting sentences in the correct order to form a story.

**Part B: Writing Test (50 Marks)**
* **Writing Paragraph:** Answering questions to form a paragraph.
* **Completing a Story:** Finishing a story from a given prompt.
* **Describing Graphs/Charts:** Analyzing and describing visual data.
* **Informal Letters/E-mails:** Personal correspondence.
* **Dialogue Writing:** Conversational English between two people.

### English 2nd Paper (Subject Code: 108)
The 2nd paper emphasizes grammar and formal composition.

**Part A: Grammar (60 Marks)**
1.  **Gap filling with clues:** Prepositions, articles, and parts of speech.
2.  **Gap filling without clues:** Prepositions, articles, and parts of speech.
3.  **Substitution Table:** Sentence making.
4.  **Right Forms of Verbs.**
5.  **Narrative Style:** Direct to Indirect speech and vice versa.
6.  **Changing Sentences:** Voice, Degree, Affirmative/Negative/Interrogative/Exclamatory/Imperative, and Simple/Complex/Compound.
7.  **Completing Sentences:** Using conditionals, gerunds, infinitives, etc.
8.  **Suffix and Prefix.**
9.  **Tag Questions.**
10. **Sentence Connectors.**
11. **Punctuation and Capitalization.**

**Part B: Composition (40 Marks)**
* **CV (Curriculum Vitae) Writing:** With a cover letter.
* **Formal Letter:** Complaint letter, notice, or application to the Principal/Headmaster.
* **Paragraph Writing:** Based on descriptive/comparative topics.
* **Composition:** A longer essay on a broad topic.

## 2. HSC English Syllabus (NCTB)

### English 1st Paper (Subject Code: 107)
Focuses on comprehension, appreciation of literature, and creative writing.

**Part A: Reading (60 Marks)**
* **Passage 1 (Seen):** MCQs and Short Answer Questions.
* **Passage 2 (Seen/Unseen):** Information Transfer/Flow Chart.
* **Passage 3 (Seen/Unseen):** Summarizing.
* **Cloze test with clues:** (Vocabulary-based).
* **Cloze test without clues:** (Vocabulary-based).
* **Rearranging sentences.**

**Part B: Writing (40 Marks)**
* **Interpreting Graph/Chart.**
* **Story Writing.**
* **Informal Letter.**
* **Creative Writing (Poem/Story/Summary based on a stimulus).**

### English 2nd Paper (Subject Code: 108)
Focuses on advanced grammar and formal writing.

**Part A: Grammar (60 Marks)**
1.  **Prepositions.**
2.  **Special uses of words/phrases:** (e.g., as if, was born, have to, let alone, as soon as).
3.  **Completing Sentences:** (Clauses and Phrases).
4.  **Right Forms of Verbs.**
5.  **Narrative Style.**
6.  **Modifiers:** (Pre-modifiers and Post-modifiers).
7.  **Sentence Connectors.**
8.  **Synonyms and Antonyms.**
9.  **Punctuation.**

**Part B: Composition (40 Marks)**
* **Formal Letter/Email.**
* **Report Writing:** For newspapers.
* **Paragraph Writing:** (Listing, Description, Comparison, Cause & Effect).
* **Free Writing/Composition.**

## 3. IBA DU Admission Syllabus (English)

The IBA (Institute of Business Administration, University of Dhaka) admission test is highly competitive and follows a pattern similar to the SAT or GMAT. There is no fixed "NCTB-style" syllabus, but the following areas are consistently tested.

### Section A: Vocabulary
* **Synonyms & Antonyms:** Advanced level vocabulary.
* **Analogy:** Identifying relationships between pairs of words.
* **Sentence Completion:** Using context clues to fill in blanks (Single and Double blanks).
* **Idioms and Phrases.**

### Section B: Grammar
* **Sentence Correction:** Identifying errors in grammar, usage, and sentence structure.
* **Subject-Verb Agreement.**
* **Dangling Modifiers.**
* **Parallelism.**
* **Tense and Conditionals.**
* **Pronoun Agreement and Reference.**

### Section C: Reading Comprehension
* **Short and Long Passages:** Testing the ability to identify the main idea, tone, inference, and specific details.

### Section D: Descriptive English (Written Part)
* **Essay Writing:** Persuasive or argumentative essays on contemporary issues.
* **Paragraph Writing.**
* **Translation:** Bengali to English and English to Bengali.
* **Precis Writing / Summarizing.**

---
*Note: The IBA syllabus is aptitude-based; practicing SAT (College Board) and Barron's GRE/GMAT materials is highly recommended.*
