**Settings**

**Theme**

Students can switch between Dark Mode, Light Mode, or System Default at any time.

Font size can be set to Small, Medium, or Large, with an option to enter a specific value for finer control.

**Sound**

Sound effects can be turned on or off globally. Students can also go deeper: each action, such as Correct Answer, Wrong Answer, Page Change, and Click, has its own sound that can be individually toggled or adjusted in volume. Multiple sound packs will be available, each with its own preset configuration. Students can apply a full pack or customize individual sounds within it.

**Notifications**

Students can set a daily reminder at a time they choose, or turn it off entirely.

**Leaderboard**

Leaderboard participation is optional. Students who want to step back from the competition can turn it off for a set period: a number of days, a number of weeks, or indefinitely until they choose to return.

While off the leaderboard, their name will not appear to others. But all XP earned during that time is still counted. If they decide to come back, their full score shows up again, including everything earned while they were away. The leaderboard reflects their real progress, just on their own schedule.

This is not a penalty system. It is a way to let students protect their focus and mental space when competition stops helping them.
